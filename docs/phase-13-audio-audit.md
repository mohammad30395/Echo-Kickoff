## Audio assets

All files are original jam-authored mono PCM16 WAV assets generated by fixed mathematical synthesis in `tools/generate_audio_assets.py`. No runtime procedural audio generation is used by production gameplay, and no third-party sound source, sample pack, AI audio service, or copyrighted game audio was used.

## Architecture

- `AudioManager` owns all imported cue streams, cue playback, bus volume state, round ambience lifecycle, Web cue logging, and user-gesture confirmation.
- `default_bus_layout.tres` defines `Master`, `Effects`, and `Ambience`; both sub-buses route to `Master`.
- `AudioSettingsPanel` is reusable in Main Menu and Pause Menu, runs while paused, and exposes independent `Master`, `Effects`, and `Ambience` sliders from `0.0` to `1.0`.
- `ListenerAudio` is a child component on the Listener scene. It plays movement cues while the Listener moves and an alert cue when the Listener begins investigating or chasing.
- Relay, decoy, echo, footstep, door, caught, and victory sounds are triggered from existing gameplay events rather than UI scenes directly coupling to gameplay scenes.
- `GameManager` stops round ambience when returning to menu or restarting, while terminal one-shots such as caught and victory are allowed to play through their transition.

## Noise hierarchy

The audible hierarchy now matches the design intent:

- Footsteps: quiet, frequent, and lower peak.
- Decoy: targeted, limited, bright impact, and louder than footsteps.
- Echo Pulse: broader and dangerous, with a distinct tonal reveal cue.
- Relay activation: longest and loudest objective cue, paired with the existing extremely loud noise event.

The test suite verifies `NoiseEvent` categories route to the matching cue IDs for footstep, echo, decoy, and relay activation.

## Accessibility and visual pairing

All audio cues are mono and not stereo-only. Gameplay-important sounds remain paired with visible state changes:

- Echo Pulse uses the existing reveal ring and HUD state.
- Decoy impact uses the trajectory, target reticle, airborne shape, impact ring, and charge HUD.
- Listener alert and movement are paired with Listener visual state and motion.
- Relay activation, door open, caught, and victory have existing shape, transition, or terminal-screen feedback.
- The audio mix can be lowered independently while keeping important cues routable at low volume.

## Performance and compatibility

- Assets are small WAV files; the largest source WAV is the 284 KB ambience loop.
- Playback uses imported `AudioStreamWAV` resources, reusable effect players by cue ID, and a single round ambience player.
- The Web build remains single-threaded and GDExtension-disabled.
- No full-screen shader, unsupported renderer feature, native extension, C#, Phaser.js, or Three.js dependency was introduced.
